## Audit and limits
`candidates.json` is the machine-readable deliverable. `sources.jsonl` retains requested/final URLs, HTTP status, UTC retrieval time, response headers, redirect history, saved-body paths, bytes and SHA256 for all attempts. `assets.json` and `f90-spec-assets.json` tie discovered manufacturer assets to their parent pages. Source originals are `.bin`; `.jpg/.png` previews/contact sheets may be derived. Only originals' SHA256 values are authoritative.54 successful response records had their byte lengths and SHA256 independently recomputed successfully (`verification.json`).

Google returned200 JavaScript shells, not useful product evidence; manufacturer store search/alternate hobby store provided primary discovery. One relative drawing fetch initially failed and was retried successfully using its manufacturer-page-resolved absolute URL. Binary image parsing produced irrelevant replacement-character warnings in automatic text sidecars; they are not used as evidence. Image-only specifications were read visually; selected rows are transcribed, full images preserved.

No formal product engineering revision/lot applicability, installed cooling model, winding-temperature ceiling, cold qualification at−20°C, rotor-clearance check, installed ESC/current path compatibility, structural fit, coupled AUW/thrust-reserve or transition/landing duty closure was established. Known stock span and manual voltage context were provided by the delegation, not independently reopened outside this directory. Manufacturer tables warn that bench values are reference-only and discourage comparison with other motor types; do not treat the heterogeneous tables as a normalized ranking or flight performance guarantee.
