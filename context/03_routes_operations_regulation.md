## Consequence of winter range
The team's own (likely optimistic) energy model gives ~**52 km winter vs ~105 km fair-weather** range at 7 kg (see `07`). So Wemotaci is a **fair-weather/road-closure service** unless a relay is added near km 40–50 of Route 25. Short island/coastal hops (≤ ~43 km) are the only routes with both documented need and winter feasibility. Outreach plan recommendation: make a winter-feasible short hop the primary design case; keep Wemotaci as the range-stretching case with a seasonal or relay caveat.

## Mission timing constraints
- CHDL (Joliette) lab accepts outside specimens Mon–Fri 8:00–16:00 and destroys most late arrivals; unspun gel tube good for 2 h at 25 °C.
- Optilab Montréal–CUSM: CBC tube within 3 h, uncentrifuged chemistry within 1 h.
- Wemotaci: ~2 h limit for unspun routine blood samples; ~80 min by drone vs 3.5–4 h by road.

## Regulation (Transport Canada, since Nov 4, 2025)
- Lower-risk BVLOS without SFOC if: pilot holds **Level 1 Complex** certificate (18+); operator holds an **RPOC**; aircraft has a **Standard 922** safety-assurance declaration; uncontrolled airspace, ≤ 400 ft AGL, **> 9.3 km (5 NM) from listed aerodromes**.
- Over sparsely populated areas (5–25 people/km²) a small RPA needs a **Pre-Validated Declaration** (C$1,200) including 922.07 reliability and 922.12 environmental envelope.
- La Tuque, Saint-Michel-des-Saints and Montmagny all have aerodromes, so clinic-end landings will likely need a PVD or SFOC.
- Lab samples are dangerous goods (UN3373); UBC DTI needed separate TDG approval.
- The −30 °C claim would eventually have to be demonstrated under 922.12.

## Operational assumptions worth modelling
- Battery swap at both ends (one-way legs).
- Hover/transition: "a minute or two" per leg; the report's factor stack uses ~3 min per leg including one go-around.
- Detour factor ×1.10 until real tracks exist.
- Reserve 20–25%.
- Aviant's Røros pilot: 46 flown, 38 cancelled (wind ≥13 m/s, fog, 4G), so reliability matters as much as range.
