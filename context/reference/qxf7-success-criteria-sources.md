# QxF7 report: sources for the Success criteria section (pages 6–9)

Our own analyses (cite as "Fumes Technologies analysis, Oct 2026"):
- Payload coverage curve (15 / 61 / 90 / 91 / 97 / 98 / 100% at 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 kg): a weighted catalogue of 16 clinic deliveries, where weight = frequency per week × value score. All values are estimates. Source chat: "Medical delivery drone payload capacity."
- Daily batch of about 0.7 kg: 100 g of tubes, 180 g of urine containers, 100 g of UN3373 packaging and a 350 g thermal box (estimate). The peak batch is about 1.2–1.3 kg.
- Thermal box calculation: a 15×10×10 cm box with 25 mm of foam (k ≈ 0.025) has U ≈ 1 W/m²K. At ΔT = 50 K the heat loss is about 4 W, or about 14 kJ per hour. That needs about 70 g of phase-change material at 200 kJ/kg, doubled for margin.
- Specimens make up about 60% of the weighted value: 32 of the 52.7 points.
- Range: 70 Canadian routes, then a Quebec-only scored ranking on six criteria (out of 45). Manawan scored 39 and Wemotaci 35. The counts within 20 / 50 / 75 / 95 km are 4 / 7 / 14 / 16 routes. Source chat: "VTOL drone optimal range."
- The winter range is roughly half the fair-weather range at the same mass (energy-model closure: 52 vs 105 km at 7 kg). The practical battery fraction is about 50%.
