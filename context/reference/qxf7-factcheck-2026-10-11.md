# QxF7 report: fact-check log (Oct 11, 2026)

These are the corrections made to the Google Doc after a claim-by-claim check, together with the new sources to use in the References section. The sources file `claude/qxf7-report-sources.md` still lists the original sources.

## Errors fixed
- **Island Lake:** the report said "the 12 km crossing remains the only way out." That was wrong, because helicopters fly directly from Wasagamack and an ice road runs in winter. It now reads "every trip out starts with a boat, helicopter or ice-road crossing." (CBC feature)
- **Manawan:** the lab-window sentence said flights could land "every day." The lab accepts samples on weekdays only, so it now says "every weekday." (CISSS Lanaudière lab page)
- **Manawan:** the road was described as "190 km, most of it on gravel." Only 78 of about 186 km is gravel, so it now reads "190 km away, 78 km of it on gravel."
- **Manawan route line:** "battery swap if necessary" was changed to "battery swap," because the 150 km trip is longer than the 95 km range.
- **Avy Aera:** the report said Avy publishes no cold rating. It does: the Aera is rated 0 to 40 °C. https://avy.eu/product/aera
- **Delivrone:** the trial was not in Normandy in 2025. It was the Air GHT trial between Amiens, Abbeville and Montreuil-sur-Mer (Somme / Pas-de-Calais), from October 2024 to January 2025. Delivrone itself is based in Rouen, Normandy. Sources: https://www.chu-amiens.fr (Air GHT), https://www.mdpi.com/2504-446X/10/1/71 and ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05885568.
- **Rigitech Eiger:** the report gave −7 °C as its rating. It is rated 0 to 40 °C and was only tested at −7 °C (Rigitech spec sheet; Rigitech LinkedIn, Feb 2025).
- **MDPI conclusion:** the paper found drone transport safe for samples (ISO 15189) but no faster than road transport, and said backup transport is imperative in bad weather.
- **UBC DTI Phase I:** this phase was operated by Drone Delivery Canada, not InDro; InDro is the Phase II partner. https://dronetransport.med.ubc.ca/phase-i/faqs/
- **Drones Express:** the claim that the health authority "lacked resources" came from the company's CEO, not from the CIUSSS. It is now attributed to the company.
- **Volatus Condor:** one aircraft was destroyed in a Q2 2023 test crash, and the program was cancelled in late 2023. The source does not say the crash caused the cancellation.

  - NTNU students who met on exchange at MIT.

  - The first flight was in March 2021.

- **Zipline:**
  - First deliveries were in Rwanda in 2016.
  - It passed 2 M deliveries in January 2026, about 1.7 M of them in Africa (as of November 2025), and US food/retail deliveries are growing fast.
  - The Ghana suspension was in November 2025, over about GH¢175 M (≈ US$15 M) in unpaid bills.
  - Use these sources instead of BusinessDay: https://www.myjoyonline.com (Zipline grounds drones) and newsghana.com.gh.
- **Wingcopter 198:**
  - Maximum range is about 94 km, less with a full load (≈75 km at 5 kg per the 2021 sheet).
  - Malawi deliveries largely stopped after donor cuts; the D+C article does not name Wingcopter.
  - It added a defence business in 2026.
- **DeltaQuad:** Aviant used "a DeltaQuad Pro" (DeltaQuad's own claim), and the #CARGO is "listed from about €10,000."
- **ECOGENE-21:**
  - The 2022 Alma demonstrations used a "small drone" (ARA Robotique).
  - The DT46 flight was "in loops" over the test range.
  - The "looking for partners" statement is dated 2024.
- **Volatus:**
  - It merged with DDC in 2024, and its HQ is now in Mirabel.
  - Its routes are "the only regular medical drone routes we found."
  - Moose Cree "stalled while funding was sought."
- **UBC Phase II:** started in July 2026. InDro "plans" flights and is "evaluating" the SkyLane 350.
- **Takeaways:**
  - The cold-rating sentence now says that every drone with a published rating stops between 0 and −20 °C.
  - The TC November 2025 rule only applies away from towns and airports, so clinic landings will need extra approval.
  - Lab samples are dangerous goods (UBC needed TDG approval).
  - Transport Canada sources: https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/drone-safety/2025-summary-changes-canada-drone-regulations and https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/drone-safety/learn-rules-you-fly-your-drone/drone-operation-categories-pilot-certificates/level-1-complex-operations
